SkyRewrite User Guide

Use SkyRewrite’s dashboard and save-scoped journal to review theft, gold, crafting, disenchanting, and brawl outcomes alongside regional memory and reputation.

Where to open it: launch SkyRewrite from SkyOS Home or SkyApps. Its dashboard is a separate SkyRewrite app. To change its settings, open SkyViewer → Modules → SkyRewrite. Dashboard preferences are stored on this device; event history is stored with the active save.

First steps

1 · Open the dashboard

  1. Open SkyOS Home or SkyApps.
  2. Select SkyRewrite.
  3. The dashboard refreshes the active save journal. If the game has just loaded, give Skyrim a moment to expose current events and loaded actors.

2 · Read the overview

Overview combines recent activity, a by-location inventory summary, recent combat traces, local pressure, and recorded Local Echo outcomes. The recent comparison uses the last 7 in-game days versus the previous 7 days. It counts recorded events; it is not a danger or prosperity rating.

Select a location where the UI offers a link to open its matching World History entries.

3 · Understand event labels

  • Consumed means a supported food, potion, or ingredient was used; potion names and learned ingredient effects are shown when available. Gold changes show the amount and current balance. Crafting materials are recorded as used for a recognized output instead of as dropped. Dropped means the item left the player's inventory for the world.
  • Picked up, purchased, looted, received as a gift, sold, stored, gifted, and confiscated describe a source or destination only when Skyrim supplies enough context. Storage can name a container, display, rack, mannequin, and available location.
  • Crafting actions include smithed, tanned, crafted potion, enchanted, disenchanted, and cooked. Materials used are linked to the resulting item and named workstation/location when available.
  • Pickpocketed and other identified theft entries appear in the Theft ledger and include item identity/FormID when available. They are separate from ordinary inventory movement.
  • Lockpicked appears after a successful lockpick of a door or container. Door entries prefer a linked house, interior, or container name when available.
  • Won brawl and lost brawl entries name the opponent when Skyrim exposes the brawl outcome; losses do not count toward win-based achievements.
  • Persuaded, intimidated, or bribed requires a matching player-selected dialogue topic and explicit Skyrim topic type. Other dialogue lines are ignored.
  • Forged records currently refers to the supported forgery action in The Numbers Job. It includes the location name when available.
  • Owning-plugin labels identify the plugin that defines a FormID; they do not prove which mod caused the action.

Dashboard tabs

Overview

Use this as the summary page. Location activity can be turned off in the module. Recent combat traces show recent player-caused kills, threshold progress, readiness, and cooldown. The pressure preview is weighted activity, not a danger score.

World History

Browse recorded travel, discovered map markers, deaths/kills, brawl wins and losses, relevant quests, inventory actions, crafting and disenchanting, equipment changes, books read, skill and level increases, learned ingredient effects, learned spells and shouts, unlocked perks, followers, and rule responses. Spell, shout, perk, and discovered-location rows show their FormID; perk rows show the skill tree when known, and discoveries show the discovered marker name and current cell when available. Potion use and brewing show the potion name when available. Disenchantment rows identify the removed item and learned enchantment when Skyrim exposes them. Crafting rows identify the output and workstation/place, and material rows are linked to that output. Use the search box and pickers to locate an event. Click Clear journal only when you intend to clear the SkyRewrite history for this save.

Theft

Review thefts separately from normal transfers. Use the All thefts, Pickpocketing, Items, and Gold filters. Search by item, NPC, location, plugin, or day. Gold is counted in septims; item quantities reflect stack counts. The view distinguishes retained rows from saved totals.

Bounties

Compare live Skyrim violent/nonviolent balances by hold with SkyRewrite temporary bounty records. The temporary ledger can filter by active, expiry due, expired, manually cleared, skipped, or verification failed; it can sort by recency, age, expiry, amount, or location. Use an individual undo only when you want SkyRewrite to attempt a safe removal. The hold clear action empties both Skyrim bounty balances for that hold.

Local Echo

Inspect recent combat traces and the weighted pressure preview. An Echo can record on revisiting a location after its threshold is met and its cooldown has ended. Review Recorded Local Echo outcomes to see whether a bounty or guard response succeeded or was skipped.

Road & Trade Memory

Select one of nine Holds or Solstheim. Regional activity totals stay available after older journal rows are pruned. Negative standing increases purchase prices; positive standing lowers them. Very negative local standing can trigger a shop robbery when you enter a loaded shop. Regional bandit defeats can improve local vendor relationships; dragon defeats can improve local guard/Jarl relationships.

Player Reputation

Review your score, title, activity counters, gifts, and nearby-reaction preview. Eligible favor quests and hostile creature defeats add standing. Theft and forgery reduce it by one; murder reduces it by five. Reputation bottoms out at −500. Gifts and relationship rewards use increasing positive thresholds.

Negative titles progress from Unwelcome (−1 to −50), to Shunned (−51 to −150), Hunted (−151 to −200), Outlaw (−201 to −350), and Public Enemy (−351 to −500).

NPC behavior

Actor Encounters groups recorded player-caused kills by actor type or placed reference. Use filters for repeated/single/multiple locations, grouping, sort order, location, plugin owner, and search. It shows recorded encounters rather than a general schedule for every NPC.

Mod Idea

Read the in-app roadmap to see which systems are active and which ideas are still planned. It is informational and has no settings to change.

Filtering World History

Category

Choose All, Travel & deaths, Quests, Inventory, Actions, or Rule responses. Travel & deaths includes discovered map markers. Actions includes learned spells, learned shouts, and unlocked perks. The Inventory choice disappears when inventory events are disabled in the SkyRewrite module.

Location and plugin owner

Choose a location or a FormID owner plugin from values found in this save's journal. Search within either picker if its list is long. A plugin owner is a record attribution, not a causal mod attribution.

Time window

Choose All retained, Last 7 in-game days, or Last 30 in-game days. Enter a custom number of in-game days in the picker (0.1–36500) for a different window.

Search

Search event text, item, quest, location, plugin, or day. Clear the search field and reset the pickers to return to the full retained history.

Inventory and crafting action wording

SkyRewrite uses source/destination references, the recently activated target, and the open game menu to describe pickups, sales, purchases, looting, gifts, storage, drops, and guard confiscation. Display cases and racks use storage labels; mannequins use a mannequin label rather than an NPC transfer. For smelting/crafting, it records materials as used for the resulting item and identifies smithing, tanning, alchemy, enchanting, or cooking when Skyrim reports the result. The station, house/interior, and hold are included when available. If Skyrim supplies no reliable partner or destination, the journal uses a neutral transfer label. A location may be a named region while a cell is the specific interior or exterior area.

SkyViewer → Modules → SkyRewrite

These switches and number fields control the SkyRewrite app. Numeric values can be typed directly within the displayed limits. The live history update delay is applied after a journal change while World history is open. SkyRewrite does not continuously poll while idle or rebuild hidden dashboard tabs.

Saved data and troubleshooting

Which data is saved?

Event history and reversible bounty records are serialized into the active save's SKSE co-save. Dashboard preferences, including the live history update delay and module-driven switches, are stored locally by the SkyRewrite page in the device/browser's local storage. The preferences and save journal are separate.

If an event or response is missing

  • Check that the relevant event category is enabled in the module and the journal has not been cleared.
  • After a recorded action, the visible dashboard refreshes once the configured quiet delay elapses; close/reopen the dashboard to request an immediate fresh snapshot.
  • Some records require a resolvable location or hold crime faction; the Bounties and World History views show failed or skipped responses when captured.
  • Guard spawning may fail when no matching hold guard is available. The outcome event explains whether a guard spawned.
  • The Numbers Job forgery hook applies to its supported ledger interaction; SkyRewrite does not infer arbitrary forged actions from unrelated mods.

Requirements and complete feature details

Requires a supported Skyrim Special Edition/Anniversary Edition runtime, SKSE, PrismaUI, and SkyOS. See the SkyRewrite Features & Reference page for behavior details and the complete reputation bands.
