SkyRewrite

Follow gold transfers, theft, crafting and disenchantment, brawl outcomes, quest and location activity, regional memory, reputation, and configured responses.

Open the user guideView every module option
What it does: SkyRewrite records supported player actions in the active save and builds explainable local and regional state from those events. It works alongside Skyrim's authored quests and systems. The dashboard is SkyRewrite's own app; its configurable preferences are in SkyViewer → Modules → SkyRewrite.

Feature reference

World History

A searchable, save-scoped timeline can include:

  • Location transitions and discovered map markers, with the marker name and current cell when available.
  • Player deaths, player-caused kills, and brawl outcomes that identify whether the player won or lost, with available actor and location details.
  • Quest starts, stage changes, and completions.
  • Learned spells, learned shouts, unlocked perks, skill increases, and player-level increases. Perk records include the associated skill tree when Skyrim's loaded perk-tree data identifies it.
  • Successful lockpicks on doors and containers; door entries use the linked house, interior, or container name when available.
  • Player-selected intimidation, bribery, persuasion, and guard-release dialogue outcomes.
  • Books read, newly learned ingredient effects, and supported potion or ingredient consumption records. Potion use and brewing show the resolved potion name when available.
  • Equipment changes and contextual item movement such as pickups, purchases, looting, gifts, sales, storage on mannequins or in displays, drops, and guard confiscation when the game event exposes enough context. Gold changes report the amount and current balance.
  • Smithing, tanning, potion making, enchanting, disenchanting, cooking, and the materials associated with a recognized crafting result. Crafting entries include the resulting item and station/place when available; disenchantment includes the removed item and learned enchantment when Skyrim exposes them.
  • Recognized thefts, crime-bounty outcomes, Local Echo responses, and regional shop robberies.
  • The Numbers Job forged-record action, with its location and crime response.

Movement labels use Skyrim's source/destination references, activation targets, and active menu context. Location details prefer available interior, house, cell, and workstation names. If the engine does not identify an NPC, transaction partner, container, or cell, SkyRewrite keeps a neutral transfer label rather than guessing. Unavailable names and plugin owners depend on the loaded game data.

Search, filters, and retention

Filter history by All, Travel & deaths, Quests, Inventory, Actions, or Rule responses. Narrow results by location, owning plugin, and time window, or search event, item, quest, location, plugin, and day. Presets include all retained entries, the last 7 days, and the last 30 in-game days; a custom window is also available.

Set the retained journal size from 512 to 2048 events. Older regular rows are removed first as the cap is reached. Compact regional totals preserve activity used by the regional and reputation panels. Clearing the journal is a separate action.

Theft and crime records

The Theft tab separates pickpocketing from item theft and septims. Pickpocket rows identify the NPC source when available; item rows include the item and FormID. These are distinct from ordinary inventory transfers and from looting a dead actor.

Recognized thefts and The Numbers Job forgery each attempt to add a 5-septim nonviolent bounty to the matching hold. A missing hold crime faction or an unchanged Skyrim balance is recorded as a failed outcome. Repeated thefts can separately trigger the optional configured temporary bounty rule.

Local Echo

When enabled, revisiting a location can record an Echo after recent weighted player-caused kills and thefts meet the pressure threshold. Activity loses weight with in-game time; the location cooldown prevents an Echo from firing on every visit. The dashboard shows pressure, readiness, cooldown, and recorded outcomes.

Optional Echo settings can show an in-game notice and add a temporary nonviolent hold bounty. Victim eligibility is configurable as non-hostile, hostile, or both. A bounty can be scaled with pressure, and has a configured amount and in-game duration.

Bounties and guard dispatch

The Bounties tab shows Skyrim's current violent and nonviolent bounty balances by hold, plus SkyRewrite temporary bounty records and their status. You can review expiry, undo an eligible temporary record, or clear the current Skyrim crime balances for a hold.

When enabled, guard dispatch attempts to spawn a matching hold guard after a SkyRewrite bounty. The event log reports dispatched guards, missing matches, or spawn failures. Guards still use Skyrim's normal AI and arrest rules; a spawn attempt does not guarantee that a guard detects or arrests the player.

Road & Trade Memory

Regional totals are tracked for the nine Skyrim Holds and Solstheim. The page compares regional hostile defeats, murders, thefts, items and septims stolen, favor quests, and related saved memory.

  • Regional reputation affects Skyrim's purchase-price base: each negative point adds 1%, capped at +500%; each positive point lowers it 1%, capped at −99%.
  • Negative regional reputation can cause a shop robbery when entering a loaded shop. The owner's non-quest stock is scattered nearby, the owner becomes angry, and the result is recorded.
  • Bandit defeats can improve local vendor relationships; dragon defeats can improve local guard and Jarl relationships when the relevant actors load.

NPC behavior

Actor Encounters summarizes player-caused kills, grouped either by actor type/base FormID or by placed reference. It includes retained counts, locations and time span, with filters for repeated, single, or multi-location encounters, grouping, sort order, location, plugin owner, and search.

It is a history of recorded player kills; it is not a general NPC schedule simulator. The separate reputation rules affect eligible nearby actors.

Overview and Mod Idea

Overview combines recent activity, a location summary, recent combat traces, local-pressure preview, and recorded Echo outcomes. The recent comparison uses the latest 7 in-game days versus the preceding 7 days. These are event counts, not danger or prosperity scores. Clicking a location can open its matching history.

Mod Idea describes the active systems and the next planned layer. It is an in-app roadmap rather than a configuration page.

Limits and interpretation

  • Skyrim's inventory-change event does not include a universal transaction reason. SkyRewrite identifies purchases/sales from barter context, looting from the recorded source, storage from a destination or recently activated display target, and drops from a player-to-world removal. Craft materials are associated with a recognized crafting result. Unclear cases remain neutral transfers.
  • A guard confiscation is labeled when Skyrim reports the guard as the transfer destination. Evidence-chest moves or scripted removals that expose no guard reference may remain generic.
  • Persuasion, intimidation, and bribery entries require a matching player dialogue-topic start/end pair and Skyrim's explicit topic subtype. Other dialogue text is not treated as an outcome.
  • Crafting labels depend on Skyrim reporting a supported crafted output and an identifiable workstation. Station and interior names are shown when the loaded references provide them; otherwise the entry uses the best available location.
  • Perks and shouts are detected by comparing the player's known forms at relevant skill-menu, quest, location, and map-discovery events. An acquisition performed by a script that raises none of these events may not appear until the next observed snapshot.
  • Owning-plugin labels identify which plugin defines a FormID. They do not identify which mod caused an action.
  • Some events need a resolved location, loaded actor, crime faction, or recognized game event. If that information is unavailable, a record or response may be partial or skipped.
  • The adaptive guard-confidence option is currently suspended for stability. Enabling its toggle does not change an actor's confidence until a safe implementation is restored.

Saved data and requirements

Where data lives

World history and SkyRewrite state are saved in the active save's SKSE co-save. Dashboard preferences are stored locally by the SkyRewrite page in this device/browser's local storage. Clearing the journal affects the save's SkyRewrite history, not the local module preferences.

Requirements

Skyrim Special Edition / Anniversary Edition on a supported runtime, SKSE, PrismaUI, and SkyOS. The SkyRewrite settings panel is provided as a module in SkyViewer. SkyRewrite is an independent app and keeps its dashboard and documentation with its own files.
