Bounded response · Local Echo

When enabled, revisiting a location records an echo when weighted pressure from recent player-caused kills meets the configured threshold within the lookback window. A per-location cooldown prevents repeats. An optional Skyrim HUD notice reports the location, weighted pressure, and contributing kill count.

Set the pressure threshold, lookback, cooldown, and notice preference in the options below. The place-memory meter and Local Echo rule use the same decay formula. Every fired rule is saved with its pressure score, threshold, and contributing kill count. Open World History and choose Rule responses to inspect fired rules and why they triggered. The optional temporary hold bounty is separately controlled in SkyRewrite settings; application and expiry outcomes are also journaled.

Actor memory

Recorded combat encounters can be grouped by actor type or by placed reference. Actor type groups combine placed references that share a base FormID; placed-reference groups keep each actor instance separate. Filter by location and plugin owner, and expand Details to see reference IDs, plugin owners, recorded locations, kill counts, and last-seen days. Older events without a base ID fall back to the reference FormID. Groups also support search, filters for repeated or multi-location activity, sorting by kills, recency, location count, or name, and links to matching journal events. This retained-journal view does not give NPCs in-game memories.

What the journal means

Inventory entries describe transfers into or out of the player's inventory; an outbound transfer does not necessarily mean an item was lost.
