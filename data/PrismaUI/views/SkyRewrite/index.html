MILESTONE 8 · NPC BEHAVIOR

Skyrim keeps its quests and authored world. SkyRewrite adds a persistent simulation alongside them: it records meaningful events, then derives local pressure and bounded consequences from the retained journal.

The journal separates pickpocketing and owned-container theft from ordinary inventory transfers. Road and Trade Memory tracks hostile defeats, civilian murders, stolen item and septim totals, and local prices for each hold. Negative reputation raises purchase prices by 1% per point, capped at 500%; positive reputation lowers them by 1% per point, capped at 99%. Murder responses intensify at 51, 101, 201, and 501 murders and clear when reputation becomes positive. Eligible quests and hostile defeats build reputation, gifts grow more valuable and frequent, and nearby friendly residents can gain relationship rank. When a hold’s reputation is negative, a local shop robbery can occur on entry: the owner’s non-quest stock is dropped around the shop and the owner becomes angry. This chance falls as regional reputation rises and stops at zero or above. Local Echo theft and bounty settings remain independent from these lasting regional records.

MOD IDEA · PROGRESS

A living record of your deeds

Built in playable layers

SkyRewrite grows alongside Skyrim’s authored quests and world. Each system is designed to remember what happened, explain why it changed, and keep consequences bounded and save-safe.

01 · JOURNALWorld history and theft

Records travel, deaths, quest progress, item transfers, pickpocketing, owned-container theft, gold, and source locations.

ACTIVE
02 · LOCAL MEMORYEchoes and bounties

Tracks recent combat pressure, records Local Echoes, can add a temporary hold bounty, and dispatches a matching guard when Skyrim has an eligible guard available.

ACTIVE
03 · PEOPLEReputation and reactions

Builds or loses reputation through eligible quests, creature defeats, theft, and murder. Residents can recognize, reward, or react to the player as thresholds are reached.

ACTIVE
04 · REGIONSTen-region memory

Tracks creature defeats, theft, murders, quest favors, shop robberies, and purchase-price changes across the nine Holds and Solstheim.

ACTIVE
05 · TRADEConsequences for local shops

Negative regional standing raises prices and can cause a shop to be robbed, with the owner reacting and goods scattered in the shop.

ACTIVE
06 · NEXTKeep widening the simulation

Build new behaviors from saved people, place, faction, and economy state while keeping triggers visible, durations bounded, and changes safely reversible.

REGIONAL SECURITY

Road and trade memory

Waiting for saved events…

Cumulative regional creature defeats, murders, and theft totals stay saved by hold after journal entries are pruned. Each hold shows its local price response: negative reputation raises purchase prices by 1% per point up to 500%, and positive reputation lowers them by 1% per point up to 99%. Negative local reputation also raises the chance of a shop robbery when you enter a loaded shop; the owner’s non-quest goods are scattered on the floor, the owner becomes angry, and the event appears in World History. The game applies the matching hold response while you are in that region.
